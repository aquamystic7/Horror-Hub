The Cooper Family

This famous black-and-white photograph allegedly shows a Texas family posing in their new home in the 1950s while a shadowy, upside-down figure drops from the ceiling. While internet lore long claimed it captured a ghost or a falling corpse, photo analysts later revealed it was a hoax created by flipping a picture of a man dangling his legs upside down and splicing it into a family snapshot.

The Ghost Boy

Taken in 1976 inside the infamous house at 112 Ocean Avenue in Amityville, New York—where Ronald DeFeo Jr. had murdered six members of his family two years earlier—this infrared photograph was captured by an automatic camera set up during a night investigation led by Ed and Lorraine Warren. The frame shows a pale young boy with glowing white eyes peering out from an upstairs doorway, whom many claimed was the spirit of murdered 9-year-old John Matthew DeFeo, though skeptics argue it was a boy from the film crew.

The Real Annabelle

Unlike the porcelain figure seen in The Conjuring films, the real Annabelle is an ordinary cloth Raggedy Ann doll purchased in 1970 for a nursing student in Connecticut. After the doll allegedly moved rooms, left handwritten notes, and scratched a visitor, paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren locked her inside a blessed wooden-and-glass case marked with a strict warning never to open it.

The Brown Lady

Captured in September 1936 by photographers from Country Life magazine at Raynham Hall in Norfolk, England, this famous image shows a veiled, glowing apparition descending the grand oak staircase. The figure is widely believed in folklore to be the spirit of Lady Dorothy Walpole, who was locked inside the mansion by her husband in the 1700s, though skeptics argue the silhouette was caused by camera shake or a double exposure.

The Bachelor's Grove

In August 1991, members of the Ghost Research Society investigated Bachelor’s Grove Cemetery near Chicago after picking up unusual electromagnetic readings. Using infrared film in an area that appeared completely empty to the naked eye, they captured a faint, translucent woman dressed in old-fashioned white clothing sitting alone on a weathered tombstone.

The Backseat Ghost

In 1959, Mabel Chinnery visited her mother's grave in Suffolk, England, and snapped a quick photo of her husband waiting alone in their parked car. When the picture was printed, a clear figure wearing glasses appeared sitting in the backseat right behind him—whom Chinnery immediately recognized as her late mother, though skeptics argue the image was caused by a double exposure or window reflection.

The Nightcrawlers

After hearing his dogs barking frantically outside in the middle of the night, a homeowner in Fresno, California, checked his backyard security camera and found footage of two pale, armless beings consisting almost entirely of long, spindly legs. The creatures glided smoothly across the dark lawn with a surreal, knee-less stride that forensic video analysts struggled to replicate with wires or puppets.

The Graveyard Ghost

A man posing in period Western attire at Tombstone's historic Boothill Cemetery in Arizona believed he was completely alone among the headstones. When the black-and-white print came back, a mysterious dark-hatted figure appeared rising out of the ground right behind his shoulder, seemingly holding a knife.

The Creeper of Waverly Hills

Inside this abandoned Kentucky tuberculosis hospital where tens of thousands of patients died, paranormal investigators snapped a dark shadow figure crawling along the floor and walls of the pitch-black fourth-floor corridor. Visitors and guides have long reported seeing this same twisted silhouette slithering across ceilings before vanishing into thin air.

The White Lady of Worstead Church

While his wife sat alone praying on a wooden pew inside St. Mary’s Church in Norfolk, England, a man snapped a photograph from a few rows back. Seated directly behind her on the empty bench was a translucent woman dressed in old-fashioned white garments and a bonnet, whom locals identify as a spectral healer said to appear to the sick.

The Paris Catacombs

Beneath the busy streets of Paris stretches a 200-mile labyrinth of pitch-black limestone tunnels lined floor-to-ceiling with the real skulls and femurs of over six million people transferred from overflowing 18th-century cemeteries. Beyond the small tourist path lies a vast, unmapped network of flooded caverns where urban explorers have gotten lost forever—and where a dropped camcorder was once recovered showing a terrified man sprinting blindly into the dark before dropping his camera.

The Hill Of Crosses

Rising out of an empty, wind-swept plain stands a dense forest of more than 100,000 wooden, iron, and stone crucifixes stacked atop one another in tangled, leaning rows. Repeatedly bulldozed and burned by Soviet authorities during the 20th century, the eerie monument kept reappearing overnight as tens of thousands of rosaries clatter softly together in the fog.
